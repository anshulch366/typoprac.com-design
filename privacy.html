2. What this starter website collects

The typing practice runs in your browser. In this starter build, we do not request your name, phone number, email address, account credentials, precise location or payment information. The typed passage and live typing calculations are handled in the page and are not sent to a server by this code.

A small local browser-storage entry may record the date of a practice session so the page can display a local streak marker. It stays on the device/browser unless the user clears site data. The site does not include analytics, advertising pixels, social widgets, external video embeds or third-party font requests.
